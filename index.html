IV

Requirements

The documents
you unroll

Each document below must be notarised and translated into Georgian before it is submitted.

  1. i

    Copy of passport

    Valid throughout the intended period of study.

  2. ii

    Graduation certificate or diploma

    School leaving certificate for bachelor's; bachelor's diploma for master's; master's diploma for doctoral.

  3. iii

    Marksheet / transcript

    The full academic record accompanying the certificate above.

  4. iv

    Photograph, 3 × 4

    Standard passport-format photograph.

  5. v

    Enrolment order

    Only for international degree mobility — issued by the university where you are currently enrolled.

Language

English at B2

A B2-level certificate is accepted. It is not required if your previous degree was taught in English — applicants without a certificate sit an English test organised by IBSU instead.

Interview

A conversation, not an exam

Every applicant completes a personal interview covering your motivation for studying in Georgia and why you chose your field.

Tuition

From approx. $2,500 / year

Fees vary by programme and cycle. Scholarships and discounts exist for strong academic records — confirm your exact figure with the IRO before applying.

V

How to apply

Five steps along
the golden road

  1. 1

    Choose your programme

    Pick the degree and cycle you are applying for, and check the intake is open.

  2. 2

    Fill the electronic application form

    The IRO issues the online form for international applicants.

  3. 3

    Send your documents

    Email the completed form with every notarised, Georgian-translated document to admissions@ibsu.edu.ge.

  4. 4

    Sit your interview

    A personal interview about your motivation and your chosen field. English testing happens here if you have no B2 certificate.

  5. 5

    Offer, visa and enrolment

    On acceptance the IRO issues your invitation documents for the student visa and residence procedure.
